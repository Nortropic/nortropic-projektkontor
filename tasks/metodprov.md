# AP07: a bounded optional method for testing an uncertain connection

Owner accepted AP07: the office will apply, separately review and integrate an
optional practical method trial. This task delivers ONE method document, not a
new checker or execution system. The office host later performs the real case
applications. This task may not claim that those applications happened or that
the method has improved productivity. Runtime performs frozen example checks,
independent review and protected integration. The result is explicitly a trial
candidate until separately judged against actual applications.

Write only tools/METODPROV.md, in clear Swedish, with these requirements:
1. Explain when a small representative pretest is useful: consequential uncertainty
   at a concrete connection with no applicable existing evidence. First read the
   actual contract and reuse valid tests. Explain when NOT needed: existing evidence
   already answers the question or reading suffices. Do not turn it into a mandatory
   gate, new trust/permission rule, framework or general audit.
2. Before trying: state the question, hypothesis, existing approach, observable
   prediction, what would contradict the hypothesis, smallest representative setup,
   what decision it can change and the boundary/stop condition. A future unavailable
   product cannot be a prerequisite of a claim to early detection.
3. Performer selects the experiment from the actual situation. The method must NOT
   disclose diagnoses, historical case names, answer keys or commands designed to
   find particular known faults. Specifically do not include sibling import/path
   or stdin-versus-arguments diagnoses/examples. These restrictions protect case
   evaluation; do not reproduce this restriction list in the document.
4. Explain use of isolated synthetic examples, preserved originals and immutable
   prior evidence; distinguish specimen defect, experiment setup fault and insufficient
   observation. Preserve ambiguous/negative results; no blind reruns or expanding
   the trial until positive. Good outcomes must not trigger needless repair/review.
5. Record actual observations, prior/after decision and additional work; compare
   with current working form which itself can use tests. Adopt/adapt/decline are
   valid. Do not equate method labels, populated fields, execution success or hashes
   with useful method effect, source truth, complete coverage or authority. Existing
   AP05/AP06 can bind sources and carry the subsequent task; they do not decide.
6. Distinguish fresh session from blinded evaluation. Preserve exact inputs and
   disclose prior knowledge/answer exposure; a historical selected case does not
   prove general effectiveness. Report available native elapsed/usage/commands if
   present, but not time savings, cost or reduced owner workload without support.
7. Give a reusable short working recipe plus ONE fully self-contained synthetic
   demonstration in a fenced python block. It must run on Python 3.12 standard
   library, require no external files, original sources, network, models or packages,
   make no writes, print exactly one JSON object with nonempty string fields
   hypothesis, observation, decision, limitation. Demonstrate a simple unrelated
   question about a numeric unit conversion assumption; assert any claimed numeric
   observation. It is an example of method use, not an automated approval tool.
8. Cite the two primary method pages below, identify their limited use and origin
   context, and distinguish that support from our unmeasured application. End with
   sufficient-result/stop rule: answer the bounded next decision or state why evidence
   is insufficient; retain authority boundaries and never grant yourself new scope.

Source summary authored and reviewed by host from read primary pages:
- IHI, https://www.ihi.org/library/tools/plan-do-study-act-pdsa-worksheet:
  describes a change-test cycle: plan, try, observe/learn, decide adjustments;
  retain records across tests. Healthcare-improvement origin. Only explanatory page
  read, no claim to downloaded worksheet or full framework.
- GOV.UK, https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-alpha-phase-works:
  recommends minimal prototypes focused on risky assumptions to inform progression;
  a whole journey need not be built. Public-service design context. No full alpha
  lifecycle, duration norm or organizational requirements adopted here.
These summaries guide method authoring, not evidence that AP07 already works.

Method fields alone cannot establish substantive correctness. The independent
reviewer must assess adequacy, boundaries and misleading effects in the prose and
example. Host application and separate outcome review are additional AP07 work.
No product code, tests, host acceptance, active office tools, Runtime or source
changes. No private identifiers/paths or raw histories. Do not include the real
case comparison or outcome; that is host-only until evaluated and content-reviewed.
